About Me

My name is Daniel Parker! I am currently employed at Altera Digital Health as an Associate Software Engineer. In my current position, I help develop and maintain our Electronic Health Record Systems that are used by healthcare providers and patients all over the world. I work on the clinical documentation that healthcare providers use to store and manage patient data. This area of our system is built using technologies in .NET Core, .NET Framework, and ASP.NET Core, so I primarily use Visual Basic, C#, and MSSQL at work.

I'm always looking for ways to grow my knowledge and skills. That's why I made this website! This website showcases all my personal projects, skills, and interests, but it was also built entirely by me using the Angular Framework. It's even self hosted and deployed on my Ubuntu server running an Ngnix web server. When you check out my photo portfolio, the images are provided by my Express and Node.Js backend.

Outside of work, I enjoy working on side projects like this website, making Unity games, and learning new programming languages. For instance, I'm currently teaching myself C++ and Vulkan by making a physics based engine. I've also been putting a lot of effort into this website.
